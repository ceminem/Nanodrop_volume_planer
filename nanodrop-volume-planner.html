NanoDrop volume planner

Paste a NanoDrop spectrum export. Each sample gets its mean absorbance over a wavelength range, and the volume needed to reach a fixed absolute amount, laid out on ibidi 8-well slides with well coordinates A1–D2 or titrated on a 96-well plate, with the IC50 from the plate readout.

Copy the whole export as it comes. Everything above the “Wavelength (nm)” line is ignored; samples are the columns after it.

Volume (µL) = absolute amount ÷ mean A over the range, both ends included.

Absolute amount per lipid

Each sample gets the amount of the lipid whose keyword appears in its name - the longest match wins, so “DOPC/DOTAP 2” is DOPC/DOTAP, not DOPC. Several keywords: separate with commas. Change a single sample in the table’s Lipid column. Samples without a lipid use the default amount; an amount typed into a well overrides both.

Plate layout

Both in triplicate. Doses go down the rows (lowest dose on top), replicates across the columns. A condition is a block of rows × columns; blocks fill the plate left to right, then the next block of rows.

Accepted: one grid with “condition dose” per well (also DOPC | 0.5); two grids one after the other, conditions first, then doses; or a list with one well per line: Well; Condition; Dose (as the CSV download). ctrl / untreated / Kontrolle = untreated control (dose 0) for every condition, DOPC ctrl or DOPC 0 only for DOPC; blank / medium / Leerwert = background, its mean is subtracted. Doses are in the unit chosen below. Wells with the same condition and dose are replicates, wherever they are.

Sample per well (µL) = dose ÷ mean A of the chosen NanoDrop sample (range set on the NanoDrop tab). Medium = final volume − sample.

Pipetting

The wells are matched to the plate layout of the 96-well titration tab: designed there as blocks, or an imported plate mask (conditions and doses per well).

4-parameter log-logistic, as drc LL.4 in R:
y = bottom + (top − bottom) / (1 + exp(slope · (ln dose − ln IC50)))
Fitted to every replicate well. IC50 is the dose halfway between the fitted top and bottom; 95 % CI by the delta method, as drc’s ED().
