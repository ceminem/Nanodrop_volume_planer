NanoDrop volume planner

Paste a NanoDrop spectrum export. Each sample gets its mean absorbance over a wavelength range, and the volume needed to reach a fixed absolute amount, laid out on ibidi 8-well slides.

Copy the whole export as it comes. Everything above the “Wavelength (nm)” line is ignored; samples are the columns after it.

Volume (µL) = target amount ÷ mean A over the range, both ends included.

Slide layout

Type a sample name into each well, or fill all slides at once. Names are matched to the NanoDrop data (case and extra spaces don’t matter), so you can set up the mask before measuring. Leave a well’s amount empty to use the target amount.
