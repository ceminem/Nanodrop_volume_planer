NanoDrop volume planner

Paste a NanoDrop spectrum export. Each sample gets its mean absorbance over a wavelength range, and the volume needed to reach a fixed absolute amount, laid out on ibidi 8-well slides with well coordinates A1–D2.

Copy the whole export as it comes. Everything above the “Wavelength (nm)” line is ignored; samples are the columns after it.

Volume (µL) = absolute amount ÷ mean A over the range, both ends included.

Absolute amount per lipid

Each sample gets the amount of the lipid whose keyword appears in its name - the longest match wins, so “DOPC/DOTAP 2” is DOPC/DOTAP, not DOPC. Several keywords: separate with commas. Change a single sample in the table’s Lipid column. Samples without a lipid use the default amount; an amount typed into a well overrides both.

Spectra

Drag the shaded range to move it, or its edges to resize it. Click anywhere else in the plot to put the single wavelength there. Point at a curve, a table row or a well to highlight that sample.

Slide layout

Drag a sample (chip below or table row) onto a well, or click a chip to put it in the next free well. Drag a well by its coordinate to move it — dropping on a filled well swaps the two. Names are matched to the NanoDrop data (case and extra spaces don’t matter), so you can set up the mask before measuring. Leave a well’s amount empty to use the target amount. Enter jumps to the next well.
